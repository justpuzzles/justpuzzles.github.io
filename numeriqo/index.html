The rules

  1. Fill the grid with 1 up to its size; no digit repeats in a row or column.
  2. The digits in each outlined cage combine to the number in its corner, using the sign beside it.
  3. − and ÷ cages have two cells, in either order. A digit may repeat inside a cage if the two cells share no row or column.

Tap a cell, then a digit. Notes pencils in the digits a cell could still take. With a keyboard: arrow keys move, digits enter, Shift with a digit makes a note, Backspace clears.

Questions

Do I ever have to guess in Keisan?

No. Every puzzle is proved to have exactly one solution before it reaches you, and each step can be reasoned out.

Is Keisan free to play here?

Yes. Today's 6×6 puzzle and new 3×3 to 5×5 grids at every difficulty are free, with no account and no ads. Grids up to 9×9, the lessons and the hints are in Numeriqo, the app.

Is today's puzzle the same as in the app?

Yes. The daily puzzle is made from the date, so this site and the app serve the same one.
