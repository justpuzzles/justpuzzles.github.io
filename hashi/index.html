The rules

  1. The number on an island is how many bridges touch it.
  2. Bridges run straight, up-down or left-right, and never cross.
  3. Two islands share at most two bridges.
  4. In the end every island can be reached from every other.

Drag from an island toward its neighbour to build a bridge, or tap the water between them. Again makes it a double, and once more clears it. With a keyboard: arrow keys move between islands, Shift with an arrow builds.

The techniques

Questions

Do I ever have to guess in Hashi?

No. Every puzzle is proved to have exactly one solution before it reaches you, and each step can be reasoned out.

Is Hashi free to play here?

Yes. Today's puzzle and new small and medium boards at every difficulty are free, with no account and no ads. Large boards, the lessons, the drills and the hints are in Just Hashi, the app.

Is today's puzzle the same as in the app?

Yes. The daily puzzle is made from the date, so this site and the app serve the same one.
