The rules

  1. Each white cell takes a digit from 1 to 9.
  2. A row or column of white cells is a run. Its clue is the run's total: top right for across, bottom left for down.
  3. A digit never repeats inside a run.

Tap a cell, then a digit. Notes pencils in the digits a cell could still take. With a keyboard: arrow keys move, digits enter, Shift with a digit makes a note, Backspace clears.

The techniques

The solver that builds each board knows 8 named techniques, listed here in the order it reaches for them. A board is kept only if they are enough to finish it.

Questions

Do I ever have to guess in Kakuro?

No. Every board is proved to have exactly one solution before it reaches you, and each step can be reasoned out.

Is Kakuro free to play here?

Yes. Today's board and new small and medium boards at every difficulty, with pencil notes, are free, with no account and no ads. Large boards, the lessons, the drills and the hints are in Just Kakuro, the app.

Is today's board the same as in the app?

Yes. The daily board is made from the date, so this site and the app serve the same one.
