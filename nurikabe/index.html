The rules

  1. Each number sits on an island of exactly that many cells.
  2. Islands never touch along an edge, and each holds one number.
  3. The sea is one connected body.
  4. The sea never fills a 2×2 block.

Tap a cell to shade it as sea. Tap again for a dot, your own note that the cell is land, and once more to clear it. Drag to paint several cells. With a keyboard: arrow keys move, Space cycles a cell.

The techniques

The solver that builds each board knows 9 named techniques, listed here in the order it reaches for them. A board is kept only if they are enough to finish it.

Questions

Do I ever have to guess in Nurikabe?

No. Every board is proved to have exactly one solution before it reaches you, and each step can be reasoned out.

Is Nurikabe free to play here?

Yes. Today's board and new 5×5 and 7×7 boards at every difficulty are free, with no account and no ads. The 10×10 boards, the lessons and the hints come with Just Nurikabe, the app, which is on its way to the App Store.

Is today's board the same as in the app?

Yes. The daily board is made from the date, so this site and the app serve the same one.
