The rules

  1. Every rectangle holds exactly one number.
  2. The number is the rectangle's area in cells.
  3. Rectangles never overlap, and no cell is left over.

Drag to draw a rectangle. Tap one to lift it. Drawing over a rectangle replaces it. With a keyboard: arrow keys, Space to start and to lay, Backspace to lift.

The techniques

The solver that builds each room knows 7 named techniques, listed here in the order it reaches for them. A room is kept only if they are enough to finish it.

  1. Ones: A 1 is its own mat
  2. Prime Strips: Primes are strips
  3. Only Fit: One fit left
  4. Common Ground: Every fit shares cells
  5. Lone Reacher: Only one clue reaches
  6. No Orphans: Don't strand a cell
  7. Count the Room: The areas don't add up

Questions

Do I ever have to guess in Shikaku?

No. Every room is proved to have exactly one solution before it reaches you, and each step can be reasoned out.

Is Shikaku free to play here?

Yes. Today's room and new 5×5 and 7×7 rooms at every difficulty are free, with no account and no ads. The 10×10 and 12×12 rooms, the lessons, the drills and the hints are in Just Shikaku, the app.

Is today's room the same as in the app?

Yes. The daily room is made from the date, so this site and the app serve the same one.
